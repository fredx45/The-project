The short version

There are no accounts and no sign-up, and no server of ours stores anything about you. Your game progress stays on your own device. Google is the only third party involved: it serves the adverts and provides the visitor statistics.

What we never collect

  • No name, email address or account of any kind — there is nothing to sign up to.
  • No precise location. Google derives an approximate region from your IP address; we never see or store your address.
  • No profile of you built by us, and nothing sold to anyone.
  • Nothing about your gameplay leaves your device except the aggregate counts described under Analytics below.

Stored on your device

The game saves your career — terms played, best score, best streak, medals unlocked and your sound preference — in your browser's local storage under the key boeGov.v1. This is not a cookie, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not readable by us. Clearing your browser data deletes it permanently. If your browser blocks storage the game still works; you simply start fresh each visit.

Advertising

Adverts are served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites on the internet. Where you have consented, those ads may be personalised to your interests.

  • Opt out of personalised advertising at Google My Ad Center.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices or Your Online Choices.
  • More detail at how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in the UK or the EEA you will be asked to consent before any non-essential cookie is set, through Google's consent message. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time using the privacy options link shown with the adverts.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 tells us how many people play, which pages they reach and where they stop. It records events such as a term being started, a term being completed with its score, a medal being earned and a result being shared, alongside device type, browser and an approximate region derived from your IP address. We read it only in aggregate and make no attempt to identify individual visitors.

  • See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles the data.
  • Opt out entirely with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or by declining non-essential cookies in the consent message.
  • Blocking or deleting cookies for this site stops the measurement and does not affect the game.

Server logs

The site is static files on a hosting provider. That provider records standard technical information — IP address, browser type, page requested — to keep the service running and secure. We do not use those logs for tracking.

Lawful basis

Advertising and analytics cookies are used on the basis of your consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Nothing else on the site processes personal data.

Children

The game suits a general audience but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access to, correction of or erasure of personal data held about you, object to or restrict its processing, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Since we hold nothing about you on any server, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, and anything held on your device can be cleared by you at any time. You are welcome to write to the contact address above with any question.

Changes

Any material change will be reflected in the "last updated" date at the top.
