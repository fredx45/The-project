The short version

This site has no accounts, no sign-up and no server that stores anything about you. Your game progress stays on your own device. The only third party involved is Google, which serves the adverts.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your career progress — terms played, best score, best streak and which medals you have unlocked — in your browser's local storage under the key boeGov.v1. It never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and clearing your browser data deletes it permanently. The game works fine if your browser blocks storage; you simply start fresh each visit.

Advertising

Adverts on this site are served by Google AdSense. To do that, Google and its partners may set cookies or read device identifiers in order to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same advert, and detect fraud. Where you have consented, these may be used to personalise the adverts you see based on your interests and prior browsing.

  • Google's practices are described at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • You can control personalisation at Google My Ad Center.
  • Third-party vendors may also use cookies; see Google's advertising policies.

If you are in the UK or the EEA, you will be asked for consent before any non-essential cookies are set, through Google's consent message. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time using the privacy options link shown with the adverts.

Server logs

The site is hosted as static files. The host records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, for security and to keep the service running. We do not have access to these logs in a form that identifies you and do not use them for tracking.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people play, which pages they reach and where they stop. Google sets cookies to distinguish one visit from another and processes information such as your approximate location (derived from IP address and not stored in full), device type, browser and the pages you view. We use it only in aggregate; we do not attempt to identify individual visitors and no personal data is sold.

  • Google's handling of this data is described at Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • You can opt out entirely with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by declining non-essential cookies in the consent message shown to UK and EEA visitors.
  • Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies for this site, which stops the measurement without affecting the game.

Our lawful basis for analytics and advertising cookies is your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or erase personal data held about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Because this site holds nothing about you on any server, most such requests are best directed to Google for advertising data, or resolved by clearing your browser storage. You are welcome to contact us at the address above with any question.
