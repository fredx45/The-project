About

A short browser game about the hardest job in British economic policy, playable in about three minutes.

What it is

Bank of England Governor puts you in charge of Bank Rate for a five-year term, starting in August 2026. Twenty quarterly decisions, two targets that fight each other, an advisory Monetary Policy Committee, and energy shocks that arrive without warning. Keep inflation between 1% and 3% and unemployment between 3% and 5% and you keep your job. Most people don't, first time.

It runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no login, and nothing is uploaded — your medals and best score are stored on your own device.

Why

Monetary policy is discussed constantly and understood rarely. Everyone has an opinion on whether the Bank should have raised rates sooner in 2021, and almost nobody has felt what it is like to make that call with a one-quarter lag, an unobservable neutral rate and a war in the news. Playing it for three minutes teaches the trade-off better than reading about it for an hour.

If you want the machinery, it is all written up in how the model works — the equations, the calibration and the things it deliberately ignores.

Accuracy

The starting conditions and the shock scale are grounded in real UK data, and the relationships are the ones central banks actually reason about. It is still a toy: six equations against an economy of 68 million people. Treat any resemblance to a forecast as coincidence, and please do not use it to trade anything.
